Four in a Row!

How to play

  1. Players take turns. Pick a word on the board.
  2. Type the past tense of that word, then press Check.
  3. The answer is revealed. If you are correct, your counter goes on the word.
  4. If you are wrong, the word is crossed out and nobody can use it.
  5. The first player to get 4 counters in a straight line wins. Rows, columns and diagonals all count!
